**Skyhaul — Island Courier** is a chase-view flight game that runs in a desktop browser from static files — no build step, no network, no libraries. Fly a courier plane over a golden-hour tropical island: a seeded terrain of beaches, mountains, a village, a lighthouse and two runways, all drawn by a hand-written pseudo-3D canvas renderer with a lit instrument panel (airspeed, altimeter, attitude ball, throttle/fuel/airframe gauges) and a compass-tape HUD.

The flight model has teeth: coordinated turns from bank, a real stall below 26 m/s, overspeed damage past 97 m/s, fuel that burns with throttle (glide it home on empty), storm banks with turbulence and hail, and landings graded smooth/firm/hard by sink rate.

**Four sorties:** (1) *Ring Run* — thread 8 golden rings against the clock; (2) *Sunset Delivery* — land a medicine crate on the village dirt strip, touchdown quality pays; (3) *Storm Patrol* — three checkpoints in a gusting crosswind between two storm banks; (4) *Harbour Rescue* — fly low over the sea and drop a pump buoy onto a lit circle beside a sinking fishing boat, scored on splash accuracy with a 150 ft altitude gate at the drop. Scores itemize rings/checkpoints, time, fuel and hull bonuses into a campaign total. A live radar in the instrument panel plots the island's real coastline, both strips, the storm banks and the active objective.

**Controls:** W/S or ↑/↓ pitch · A/D or ←/→ bank · Shift/Q or mouse wheel throttle · mouse steers · Space brakes on the ground · F drops the buoy (Harbour Rescue) · M mute.
